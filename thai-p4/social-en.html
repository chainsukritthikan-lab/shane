Key vocabulary from the guide

Say each word and its meaning out loud before the test

Citizenship

Coexistence: living together
Co-operate: work together toward a shared goal
Good leader: listens, is fair, accepts responsibility
Good follower: cooperates, listens, completes duties

Children's rights

Guarantee: promise or assurance
Exploitation: unfair use of another person for benefit
Four rights: life and development, education, protection, family and care

Democracy

Sovereign power: highest authority of a state
Constitution: fundamental principles and rules of government
Eligible: having the required qualifications
Thailand: a constitutional monarchy

Religion and morals

Wisdom: knowledge and good judgment
Moral: about right and wrong
Principle: a basic rule or belief
Ordain: formally enter a religious order
Sermon: a religious or moral talk
Doctrine: accepted teachings
Defilement: an impurity or negative mental quality
